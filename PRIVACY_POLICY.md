# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 12, 2026

## Purpose

in2md is a Firefox extension that extracts LinkedIn content (user profiles, posts, and comments) as Markdown and blocks LinkedIn's feed autorefresh.

## Data Collection

We value your privacy and collect as little data as possible.

- **No data is collected, stored, or transmitted.** All processing occurs entirely within your browser. Extracted content is either copied to your clipboard or saved as a local file — nothing is sent to external servers.

## Permissions

The permissions requested by this extension are used solely for its intended functionality:

- **activeTab** — allows the extension to read content from the active LinkedIn tab when you click an extraction button.
- **clipboardWrite** — allows the extension to copy extracted Markdown to your clipboard.

These permissions do **not** grant access to personal data or browsing activity beyond what is necessary for operation.

## Third-Party Access

This extension does **not** use analytics, tracking scripts, advertising services, or any other third-party integrations.

## Children's Privacy

The extension does not knowingly collect or store any data related to minors.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements or regulatory requirements. Updates will be posted on the Firefox Add-ons listing page and/or this repository.
